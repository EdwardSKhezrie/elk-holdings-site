The Firm

A family office built to move when others wait.

ELK Holdings is a family office. We invest across real estate, fixed income, private equity, venture capital, operating businesses, service and trade businesses and public equities, and treat every category as a core business. In each one we look for the same thing: situations where the price has fallen behind the value.

The firm grew out of LKE Group, founded by Eddie Khezrie in New Jersey in January 2026, and draws on the Khezrie family's long history of building and running businesses. That background shapes how we invest: we think like owners and operators, not just buyers.

We are a partnership, not a fund. Our partners know the deal, see the progress, and hear from us every month. We put our own capital in alongside theirs, and we only make money when they make money.

Our Fundamentals

How we work with partners

Investing with ELK should feel like owning the deal yourself, not handing money to a big fund and hoping for an update. These are the rules we hold ourselves to on every investment.

01 · Aligned

We win when you win

We are not interested in making money before our partners do. When everyone in the deal is making money, that is when we make money.

02 · Always informed

You always know where your money is

Regular updates, photos, documents and current numbers on a private portal built for each partner. Good news or bad, you hear it from us first.

03 · Committed

We strike fast and we follow through

When we are ready to buy, we move quickly and decisively. We do not back out of deals we commit to, and sellers and brokers know it.

04 · Honest

Straight answers, every time

We tell partners what we see, including the risks. Trust is built over many deals, and we plan on doing many deals together.

05 · Disciplined

Downside first

Every deal is tested against the worst case before the best case. If the numbers only work when everything goes right, we pass.

06 · Hands-on

In the details

We visit the property or the business, talk to the tenants, managers and customers, and stay close to the work from the day we invest to the day we exit.

Our Process

From first look to full value

Every investment follows the same path. Each step has one job: make sure we buy right and finish strong.

  1. I

    Source

    Direct relationships with brokers, bankers, lenders and business owners nationwide, including off-market opportunities.

  2. II

    Underwrite

    Financials, leases, collateral and costs, all tested against the downside before we make an offer.

  3. III

    Acquire

    When we are ready, we strike fast with a clear offer, and we do not back out once we commit.

  4. IV

    Execute

    A written plan for each investment, managed hands-on, whether that means leasing a building, collecting on a loan or growing a company.

  5. V

    Realize

    Sale, refinance, repayment or long-term hold, timed to return capital and maximize value for partners.

When my partners are making money, that is when I am making money. Not before.

Leadership

Meet the founder

Founder & Chief Executive OfficerEddie Khezrie

Motivated, hands-on, and focused on building relationships that last longer than any one deal.

At 22, Eddie Khezrie is a driven investor who wants to make deals, build real relationships, and outwork everyone in the room. Deal-making is in his blood. He grew up around the Khezrie family businesses and learned early how deals get made and how partners should be treated.

He sharpened that instinct across lending, underwriting and management at Safra National Bank, Kushner and 601W Companies, and today oversees real estate investments at Global Group Acquisitions.

His approach is simple. Be honest. Keep partners updated, always. Create real value, and make sure everyone at the table does well. Eddie is not looking to make money before anyone else does. He makes money when his partners make money, and he wants every partner to feel like part of the investment, not a name in a fund.

Having invested in other companies and deals ourselves, we know what we wanted as investors and often did not get: real transparency. So that is what ELK gives its partners. We are fully open about every deal, and our partner portal shows each investor exactly where their money is, what is happening on the property, and what it is worth today.
